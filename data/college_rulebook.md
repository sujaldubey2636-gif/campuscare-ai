# CampusCare Official College Rulebook

## Finance and Fee Refunds
- Semester fees are fully refundable only within the first 15 days of the academic session.
- After 15 days, no refunds will be issued under any circumstances.
- Hostel and Mess fees are strictly non-refundable once the room is allocated.
- Duplicate fee payments are processed and refunded automatically within 7-10 business days after bank verification.

## Examination and Attendance
- A minimum of 75% attendance is strictly required to appear for end-semester examinations.
- Medical leaves require a certified doctor's note submitted within 3 days of returning to campus.
- Re-evaluation of answer scripts must be applied for within 7 days of result declaration along with a fee of Rs. 500 per subject.

## Hostel and Accommodation
- Campus curfew is strictly 10:00 PM. Late entries require prior permission from the Chief Warden.
- Damage to hostel property or equipment will result in fines deducted directly from the security deposit.
- Changing allocated hostel rooms is not permitted without written approval from the Hostel Administration.

## Library Rules
- Books can be issued for a maximum of 14 days.
- Late returns will incur a fine of Rs. 10 per day.
- Lost books must be replaced with the exact same edition or a fine of 150% of the book's cost will be charged.

## IT and Network Access
- Student Wi-Fi is restricted to academic use. Torrenting or illegal streaming will result in an immediate and permanent network ban.
- Network credentials (passwords) must not be shared. 
- Wi-Fi limits are capped at 5GB per day per student.
